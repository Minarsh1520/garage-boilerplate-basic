# Leave Policy

This policy explains how employees request leave at Northbridge Digital. It is fictional demo content created for the RMIT AI Assistant for HR project.

## Types of leave
Full-time employees receive annual leave, personal (sick and carer's) leave, and compassionate leave. Leave balances are shown in the Employee Portal under My Leave. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.

## Requesting leave
Submit leave requests in the Employee Portal under My Leave. For planned annual leave, submit your request at least two weeks before the first day of leave. Include the dates and the type of leave. For unplanned personal leave, notify your manager as soon as possible on the day, then submit the request in the portal when you return.

## Who to contact
Contact your manager first about planning leave around team commitments. Contact HR at hr@northbridge.example for questions about leave balances, entitlements or leave types not covered here.

## Approval process
Your manager reviews each leave request in the Employee Portal and approves or declines it, usually within three business days. You receive an email when a decision is made. The AI Assistant can explain this process, but it cannot approve, decline or change leave requests.
