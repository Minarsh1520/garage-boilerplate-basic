# New Employee Onboarding Guide

This guide explains what new employees at Northbridge Digital need to complete during onboarding. It is fictional demo content created for the RMIT AI Assistant for HR project.

## Documents required
Before your first day, submit the following through the Employee Portal:
- Personal Details Form, including your address, phone number and emergency contact.
- Tax File Number (TFN) Declaration, so payroll can tax your pay correctly.
- Proof of identity, such as a passport or driver licence, which HR sights on your first day.

Your bank account details for pay are entered by you directly in the Payroll section of the Employee Portal. Never send bank details by email or chat.

## Onboarding task deadlines
All deadlines count from your start date.
- Personal Details Form: before your first day.
- TFN Declaration: before your first day.
- IT account activation: on your first day, during your IT setup session.
- Workplace Health and Safety (WHS) Induction module: before your first day.
- Company Policies acknowledgement: within 14 days of your start date.
- Super Fund Nomination: within 28 days of your start date.
- All remaining mandatory training modules: within 30 days of your start date.

If you cannot meet a deadline, tell your manager and HR as early as possible.

## First-day information
Your first day starts at 9:00 am at the reception desk on Level 3, 200 Collins Street, Melbourne. Bring your photo ID. Your manager meets you at reception and introduces you to your team. At 10:00 am you attend an IT setup session where your laptop is issued and your IT account is activated. Lunch is provided on your first day.

## Induction process
Induction runs across your first week. Day one covers your team, workspace and IT setup. During the rest of the week you meet with HR for a policies overview, complete a building safety walkthrough, and have a one-on-one with your manager to agree your first 30-day goals. At the end of week one, your manager checks in to answer any questions.

## Mandatory training
Mandatory training is completed online through the Learning Hub. There are three modules:
1. Workplace Health and Safety (WHS) Induction, which must be completed before your first day.
2. Privacy and Information Security.
3. Respect at Work.

Modules 2 and 3 must be completed within 30 days of your start date. Each module takes about 45 minutes. Completion is recorded automatically in the Learning Hub and updates your onboarding checklist.

## Onboarding checklist expectations
Your onboarding checklist on the Home page shows every onboarding task and whether it is completed or pending. Tasks update automatically when the related form or training is submitted. Aim to complete the tasks with the earliest deadlines first. If a task shows as pending but you believe you have completed it, contact HR.
