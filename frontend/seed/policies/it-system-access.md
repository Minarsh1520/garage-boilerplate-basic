# IT & System Access Guide

This guide explains how new employees get set up with IT equipment and system access at Northbridge Digital. It is fictional demo content created for the RMIT AI Assistant for HR project.

## Employee account setup
Your Northbridge account is created before your first day and activated during the IT setup session at 10:00 am on your first day. You receive a temporary password in person and must change it at first login. Your account gives you access to email, the Employee Portal and the Learning Hub.

## Laptop and device setup
Your laptop is issued at the IT setup session on your first day. It comes with the standard software already installed. You must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on your phone during the session using the Northbridge Authenticator app. Personal devices may only access work email through the approved mobile app.

## Access to required systems
Standard access covers email, the Employee Portal, the Learning Hub and team chat. Access to other systems your role needs, such as code repositories or finance tools, is requested by your manager through an IT access request. Most requests are completed within two business days.

## Password and access problems
If you forget your password, use the self-service reset link on the login page. If you are locked out after five failed attempts, wait 15 minutes or contact the IT Service Desk. Never share your password or MFA codes with anyone, including IT staff.

## Contacting IT Support
The IT Service Desk is available Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 6:00 pm.
